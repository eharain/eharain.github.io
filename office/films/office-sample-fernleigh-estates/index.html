    Fernleigh Estates: a free estate and letting agent database

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Fernleigh Estates is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented estate and letting agent that sells homes, lets them and manages them for landlords. This film shows a home with its viewings and offers, the applicants who match it, sales by stage, homes slow to sell and tenancies ending. It prints viewing confirmations, memoranda of sale, tenancy agreements, landlord statements and commission invoices. Every name and figure in it is made up.
