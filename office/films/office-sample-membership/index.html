    Membership: a free ready-made members database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Membership is one of the five ready-made databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an empty list of members, what they have paid and the renewals coming due. This film picks it on the start screen, types members and a payment into their forms, shows the renewals due query and the payments by member report, and adds a field of your own. Everyone typed in is invented. Download Rutba Office: https://office.rutba.io/download
