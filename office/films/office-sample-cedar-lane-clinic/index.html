    Cedar Lane Clinic: a free private clinic database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Cedar Lane Clinic is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented private clinic that bills its patients and their insurers. This film shows an appointment and its visit note, the day sheet, how full each diary is, no-shows, recalls due and money owed. It prints appointment letters with the policy reference, recall letters, invoices to insurers, payment receipts and insurer statements. Every patient, note and insurer in it is made up.
