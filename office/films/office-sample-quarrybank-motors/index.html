    Quarrybank Motors: a free garage database for MOTs and services

    Published 24 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Quarrybank Motors is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented garage doing MOTs, services and repairs. This film shows a car and its MOT history, the MOTs and services coming due, reminders to send, advisories nobody has fixed yet and the open job cards. It prints estimates, job cards, invoices that say the MOT fee carries no VAT, reminder letters and trade statements. Every name, make and figure in it is made up.
