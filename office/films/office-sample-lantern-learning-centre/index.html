    Lantern Learning Centre: a free tuition centre database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 24 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Lantern Learning Centre is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented tuition centre with after-school classes, holiday courses and evening classes. This film follows one family through a term: their children, a class and its register, attendance below eighty-five percent, fees outstanding and progress. It prints enrolment letters, one fee invoice for the whole family, receipts, term reports, certificates and teachers' pay statements. Every family, child and teacher in it is made up.
