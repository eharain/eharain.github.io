    Hazel Row Flowers: a free florist database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Hazel Row Flowers is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented florist with a shop counter, deliveries, weekly contracts and weddings. Each bouquet has a recipe of stems, which works out both its margin and what to buy at the market. This film shows the stems to buy, the delivery run, waste by week and the weddings coming up, then the order confirmation, gift card, delivery notes, wedding quotes and invoices it prints. Every name and figure in it is made up. Download Rutba Office: https://office.rutba.io/download
