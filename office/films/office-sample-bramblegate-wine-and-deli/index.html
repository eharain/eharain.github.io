    Bramblegate Wine and Deli: a free database for a shop that sells by date

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Bramblegate Wine and Deli is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented deli and wine shop where stock lives in batches, sold earliest date first. This film follows the morning date check, markdowns and write-offs, and the age checks on alcohol sales. It prints the markdown label sheet, which says so on a day with nothing to reduce, yellow-sticker labels, till receipts, the refusals register for the licence and purchase orders. Every name and figure in it is made up, and no real drinks brand appears. Download Rutba Office: https://office.rutba.io/download
