    Tannery Yard Fitness: a free gym membership database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Tannery Yard Fitness is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented gym with members on monthly plans, a class timetable and personal trainers. This film shows a new member and their plan, classes with places, the class register, failed direct debits to chase, members not seen for a month and training packs running low. It prints welcome letters, membership cards, agreements, monthly invoices worded by their payment status and pack receipts. Every name and figure in it is made up.
