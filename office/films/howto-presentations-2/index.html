    Pictures and shapes in Rutba Office Presentations, moved and sized by hand

    Published 24 Oct 2026 · How-to guides

    How to put a picture and a shape on a slide in Rutba Office Presentations, then move, size, turn and line them up. This episode inserts a picture from Insert, Pictures, drags it into place, sizes it from a corner and turns it in steps of fifteen degrees with Shift held. It adds a rounded rectangle, types a label into it, styles it with Quick Styles and centres it on the slide from Arrange, with Align to Slide ticked. It ends with Bring forward and Send backward for things that overlap. The shop and its loyalty card are made up. Download Rutba Office: https://office.rutba.io/download
