    Rutba Office Contacts: a free address book on your own computer

    Published 21 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Contacts in Rutba Office opens the address book you export from another program, a .vcf or a .csv, and keeps it on your own computer. Add as many emails and numbers as someone has, and sync with a CardDAV server when you want them on your other devices. Free, with no account. Download: https://office.rutba.io/download
