    Ironbridge Repairs: a free repair shop database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Ironbridge Repairs is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented repair shop for phones, tablets, laptops and small appliances. This film follows a job from booking-in to collection, with the job board, jobs awaiting parts or past their promised date, repairs ready and uncollected, and each technician's turnaround. It prints the booking-in receipt, the quote, the invoice, the collection receipt, a ninety-day warranty certificate and account statements. Every name, make and figure in it is made up.
