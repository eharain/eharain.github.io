    What's new in Rutba Office 2.18: Documents, Worksheets and Data Files

    Published 27 Oct 2026 · What's new

    Rutba Office 2.18 brings the most changes to Documents, Worksheets and Data Files. In Documents, a comment belongs to the words it is about, tracked changes are reviewed one at a time, and a style is changed from the gallery with Modify Style. Worksheets gains filter arrows, charts that redraw as their cells change, a Chart Design tab and a Filters area in PivotTable Fields. In Data Files, a form's fields are moved and sized in Design, subform lines are changed where they stand, a report chooses its totals, and a table can be read from a CSV file, a spreadsheet, another database or a web address. Chart Design does not set the axes or the legend yet, and a chart inside a group does not follow its cells yet. The shop, its staff and its figures are made up.
