    What's new in Rutba Office 2.22: Live Broadcast, a free app for going live

    Published 3 Nov 2026 · What's new

    Rutba Office 2.22 adds a fourteenth app, Live Broadcast, for going live with several cameras. Each camera gets a shot of its own, Preview keeps the next shot ready until you Cut or Fade to it, and Follow the speaker brings up whoever is talking, by the microphone paired with each shot. Rotate moves between the shots on a timer. It streams to YouTube, Facebook, Twitch or your own RTMP server at once, with the stream keys kept encrypted on your computer. The Recorder it grew out of is rebuilt as one studio, where a camera is handled just as a screen or a window is. AI Tools has a Recorder row, Off until you choose, so an AI app on this computer can record a window into a folder you allow. Every app's menu now has the same order. Also fixed: mail a server had already filed in its Junk folder now shows, Data Files receipts show the table's own number, and a long list's matches on a Data Files form now come in front of the fields under them. Free for home and business, with no account. Download: https://office.rutba.io/download
