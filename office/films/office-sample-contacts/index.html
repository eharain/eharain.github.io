    Contacts: a free ready-made address book in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Contacts is one of the five ready-made databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an empty address book for your own people. This film picks it on the start screen, types a few contacts into its form, shows the contact list query and the printed phone list, and adds a field of your own in the table's design.
