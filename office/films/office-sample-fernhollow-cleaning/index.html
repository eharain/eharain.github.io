    Fernhollow Cleaning: a free contract cleaning database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Fernhollow Cleaning is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented cleaning firm with office contracts, one-off jobs and a team of cleaners. This film shows a contract and its tasks, the week's rota, missed or short visits, what each contract earns against the wages, renewals and work to invoice. It prints the cleaning contract, site visit sheets, quotes, monthly invoices and payslips. Every name and figure in it is made up. Download Rutba Office: https://office.rutba.io/download
