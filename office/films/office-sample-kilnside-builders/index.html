    Kilnside Builders: a free database for a building firm in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Kilnside Builders is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: an invented building firm doing extensions, lofts and kitchens. This film follows one job through its stages, then the enquiries to follow up, stage payments due, variations awaiting agreement, retentions due for release and job profit. It prints the quotation in sections, the contract, stage invoices with the retention taken off, variation orders and statements. Every name and figure in it is made up.
