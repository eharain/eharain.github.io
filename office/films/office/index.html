    Rutba Office: free, open-source office apps for Windows, in one download

    Published 19 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Made when Rutba Office had ten apps; it now has thirteen. Talk signs in with a free Rutba account; every other app needs no account.

    Here is a short introduction to Rutba Office, a free office suite for Windows. One download gives you ten apps: Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Documents, Worksheets, Presentations, PDF, Pictures, Image and Video.

    There is no account to make, and everything keeps working with the network unplugged. It reads and writes the Microsoft Office formats and opens your old Outlook and Mac mail archives. It is free for home and business use, and the code is open source.
