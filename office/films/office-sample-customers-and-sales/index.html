    Customers and sales: a free ready-made customer database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Customers and sales is one of the five ready-made databases in Rutba Office Data Files: the companies you sell to, the people there and the deals in hand. This film picks it on the start screen, types companies, a contact, two deals and a call into their forms, shows the open opportunities query and the pipeline by company report, and adds a field of your own. Every company and person typed in is invented. Download Rutba Office: https://office.rutba.io/download
