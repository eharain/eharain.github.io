    Rutba Office Locker: lock a folder where it already is, for free

    Published 13 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    The Locker is a new app in Rutba Office 2.24. Choose a folder and its files are encrypted one by one, in the place they already are, so a cloud drive or a stolen laptop holds nothing anyone can read. You make a password and get a recovery key, shown only once. If you forget the password and have no recovery key, the files cannot be opened, and nobody at Rutba can get them back. A file over 50 MB is kept in pieces, so a cloud drive uploads only the parts that changed. New files wait as not locked until you lock them. Open a locked file from the Locker and what you save is locked again. Send to someone locks a copy with a password of its own. New to the Locker? Start with a copy of a folder. Lock it and let it finish, then close the Locker, open it again with your password and check your files are all there. Write your recovery key down and keep it safe. Once you know you can always get back in, lock the folders that matter. Free for home and business, with no account. Download: https://office.rutba.io/download
