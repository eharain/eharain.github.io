    Rutba Office Mail: automatic replies that keep going with your computer off

    Published 22 Oct 2026 · What's new

    In Rutba Office 2.14, Mail's Out of office turns on your mail server's own automatic reply, so it answers while your computer is off. While it is on, a line at the foot of the window says so, with Turn off. Where a server cannot do it, such as an imported mailbox, Mail says why and sends the replies itself while it runs. It works with most mail servers that use Dovecot, Rutba's own included. Free, with no account. Download: https://office.rutba.io/download
