    Make a chart from a range in Rutba Office Worksheets

    Published 28 Oct 2026 · How-to guides

    How to make charts in Rutba Office Worksheets. This episode selects a shop's takings for January to March with their headings, makes a column chart, moves it and gives it a title on the Chart Design tab, draws the same weeks as a line, shows the year's totals by department as a pie, changes a figure to show the chart following it, and puts a line sparkline in a cell beside each week. The shop and its takings are made up for the film. Download Rutba Office: https://office.rutba.io/download
