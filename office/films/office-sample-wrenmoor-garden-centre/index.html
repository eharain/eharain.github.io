    Wrenmoor Garden Centre: a free staff and payroll database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 24 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Wrenmoor Garden Centre is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files, for a business with staff: about thirty people at an invented garden centre. This film covers employees and their terms, holiday requests, who is off this week, timesheets to approve, payroll cost by department, and the payslip, timesheet, leave request, contract summary and annual pay summary it prints. Its tax, social insurance and pension rates are invented examples, not any country's rules, so replace them with your own country's or your payroll provider's figures before paying anyone.
