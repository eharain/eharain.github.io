    Templates in Rutba Office Documents: start from one and keep your own

    Published 24 Oct 2026 · How-to guides

    How to start a document from a template in Rutba Office Documents, and keep a template of your own. This episode opens the template gallery from the home screen and starts a letter from the plain letter template. It types a shop's name over the example, puts its address in the footer, and saves the letter as a Word template in Custom Office Templates. Then it opens the new template again from the home screen and the gallery, and the template itself stays as it was. The shop, Larkspur Corner Store, and everyone in it are made up.
