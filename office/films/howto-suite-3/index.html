    How Rutba Office updates, and where your files stay

    Published 13 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    How updates work in Rutba Office: it checks in the background, downloads the new version, and asks you to restart, or installs when you quit. You can turn the check off. Your documents stay where you save them, with no account. The update check and a daily check for announcements are the only requests Office makes by itself, and each has its own switch in About.
