    Read Aloud in Rutba Office: Documents, Slides and PDF

    Published 24 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    How to have Rutba Office read your work aloud. Read Aloud reads a sentence at a time, with a bar to pause, go back or forward a sentence, change the speed and choose the voice. In Documents each sentence is selected as it is read, in Slides it reads the slide, its notes, or both, and in PDF it reads from the page shown and turns the pages as it goes. Speech is made on this computer with a voice you add in Voices and languages, and what it reads never leaves it. The handbook and the deck are made up. Free for home and business, with no account.
