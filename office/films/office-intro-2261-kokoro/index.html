    Rutba Office: fifteen free office apps for Windows, in one download

    Published 19 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    Here is a short introduction to Rutba Office 2.26, a free office suite for Windows. One download gives you fifteen apps, and you choose which ones to install.

    Office: Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Talk, Documents, Worksheets, Presentations, PDF, Data Files and the Locker. Studio: Pictures, Image, Video, Recorder and Live Broadcast. Five of them are new since our first film: Talk, Data Files, the Locker, the Recorder and Live Broadcast.

    Fourteen of the apps need no account, and they keep working with the network switched off. Talk, for messages and calls, signs in with a free Rutba account. Rutba Office reads and writes the Microsoft Office formats and opens your old Outlook and Mac mail archives. It is free for home and business use, and the code is open source.

    New to the Locker? Start with a copy of a folder. Lock it and let it finish, then close the Locker, open it again with your password and check your files are all there. Write your recovery key down and keep it safe. Once you know you can always get back in, lock the folders that matter.

    The film was put together in Rutba Office's own Video app. The shop, the people and the files are made up.
