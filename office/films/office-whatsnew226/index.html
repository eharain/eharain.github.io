    What's new in Rutba Office 2.26: Locker files in memory, a safety copy, and voices

    Published 19 Nov 2026 · What's new

    Rutba Office 2.26 opens a locked file from the Locker in memory, with no plain copy on the disk, and can keep a safety copy of a folder until you say all is good. Voices and languages are now add-ons you choose, Read Aloud is in Mail, Documents, Slides and PDF, and Video, the Recorder and Live Broadcast can turn typed words into a voice. Every word of this film was spoken by Rutba Office's own Text to speech, made on this computer. The shop, the people and the files are made up. New to the Locker? Start with a copy of a folder. Lock it and let it finish, then close the Locker, open it again with your password and check your files are all there. Write your recovery key down and keep it safe. Once you know you can always get back in, lock the folders that matter. Free for home and business, with no account.
