    Copperfield House: a free front desk database in Rutba Office Data Files

    Published 23 Oct 2026 · Sample databases

    Copperfield House is one of the free sample databases in Rutba Office Data Files: the front desk of an invented office building, with nine companies on seven floors. This film follows a visitor from booking to badge, then the lists the desk keeps: who is in the building now, who is expected, badges not returned, contractor inductions and parcels waiting. When the alarm sounds, the fire roll call list prints a page for each floor, and the print templates cover visitor passes, site rules, induction certificates, delivery slips and the roll call record. Every name and record in it is made up. Download Rutba Office: https://office.rutba.io/download
