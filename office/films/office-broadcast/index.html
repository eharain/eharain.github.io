    Rutba Office Live Broadcast: go live with several cameras, for free

    Published 3 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    Live Broadcast is the live streaming studio in Rutba Office, new in 2.22. Each camera gets a shot of its own, with a wide shot of everyone, and Preview keeps the next shot beside the one on air until you Cut or Fade to it. Follow the speaker brings up whoever is talking, by the microphone paired with each shot, and Rotate moves between the shots on a timer. Stream to YouTube, Facebook, Twitch or your own RTMP server at once, with the stream keys kept encrypted on your computer. Free, with no account: tick it when you install, or add it from Choose apps. Download: https://office.rutba.io/download
