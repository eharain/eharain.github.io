Rutba Sign for business: agreements signed in person, with the evidence

Kilnside Builders, a building firm in Bristol, agrees every job in writing in Rutba Sign before the work starts. The agreements come from the starter library: a services agreement for customers and a freelance agreement for the trades. Each envelope keeps an evidence record, step by step and chained, and the agreements list keeps the dates. At a customer's house, the builder hosts the signing in person on a tablet: the customer agrees to sign electronically, fills in the work and the price, types a name and signs, and the agreement is sealed on the spot.
