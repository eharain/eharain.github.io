    Rutba Dispatch: the basics

    Published 3 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba Dispatch runs the deliveries: what goes out, on which trip and with which rider, and the cash that comes back. A short tour with Saffron Lane Pizza and Curry, an invented takeaway in south London, on the morning after a Monday evening's service: the day's board, last night's trips, one rider's run stop by stop with its cash, the cash bags, the consignments, the delivery zones and service levels, and thirty days of numbers. Nothing is saved or moved in the film. Saffron Lane Pizza and Curry is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/orders/dispatch
