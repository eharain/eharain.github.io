    Rutba Track: the basics

    Published 3 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba Track is the page a customer opens from the link in their delivery message: one delivery, no account and nothing to sign in to. A short tour with Saffron Lane Pizza and Curry, an invented takeaway in south London: a delivery number on its own refused, a live delivery with its expected time and the stops still ahead, what the page leaves out because links get forwarded, a delivered order with its proof, and the one-tap rating. Nothing is saved in the film. Saffron Lane Pizza and Curry is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/orders
