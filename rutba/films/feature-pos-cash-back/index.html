    Rutba Point of Sale: cash back at the till

    Published 19 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    New in Rutba Point of Sale: cash back on mobile wallet and bank payments. At Larkspur Corner Store, Wale Dubois rings up croissants and milk, the customer pays by mobile wallet and takes twenty pounds in cash, and the till adds the fee to what she sends. The register counts the cash that left the drawer against what should be there at closing. Cash back is offered only to people allowed to give it, within the shop's limits. Larkspur Corner Store is an invented corner shop in Leeds, made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/commerce/pos
