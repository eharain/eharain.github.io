    Rutba Procurement: the basics

    Published 2 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Procurement is the buying half of Rutba Inventory: requests and their approvals, quotes from suppliers, contracts and prices, and the check that an order, its delivery and its bill agree. A short tour with Northbank Trading, an invented wholesaler in London. A request for Christmas stock waits on its second approval, three suppliers are asked to quote for jasmine rice, and one supplier's insurance has run out. Then a spice contract and its prices, purchase orders, a delivery notice, an invoice match that found two problems, and a year of spend. Northbank Trading is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/inventory/procurement
