    Rutba Relay: publish once, every platform answers for itself

    Published 19 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Write a post once and choose where it goes. Rutba Relay publishes it to every platform in the background and reports on each one separately.

    Each platform has its own limits, so Relay fits the post to each one before anything is queued and tells you exactly what it changed. A slow platform never holds up the others, and a retry cannot post the same thing twice.

    Teams get a console with a calendar that understands time zones, and an approval step. Developers get one API with signed webhooks, and the same tools can be handed to an AIAgent.

    It is free to start, with up to 30 posts a month.
