    Rutba Sign for business: agreements signed in person, with the evidence

    Published 31 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Kilnside Builders, a building firm in Bristol, agrees every job in writing in Rutba Sign before the work starts. The agreements come from the starter library: a services agreement for customers and a freelance agreement for the trades. Each envelope keeps an evidence record, step by step and chained, and the agreements list keeps the dates. At a customer's house, the builder hosts the signing in person on a tablet: the customer agrees to sign electronically, fills in the work and the price, types a name and signs, and the agreement is sealed on the spot. Anyone can then check the record from its code. Kilnside Builders is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/sign
