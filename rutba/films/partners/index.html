    Rutba Partners: sell Rutba where you already have the relationships

    Published 19 Oct 2026 · Partners

    We build the software, and we would rather you sold it in your market.

    The Rutba partner programme has four tracks. A referral partner makes the introduction and is paid a fee on what the customer is invoiced. A reseller buys at partner pricing and sells at their own. Implementation partners bill setup and training at their own rates and keep all of it. A country distributor runs a whole territory and can appoint partners beneath them.

    There is nothing to pay to join. Eleven territories are open and none is spoken for yet. Registering interest is eight fields.
