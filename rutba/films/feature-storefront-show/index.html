    Rutba Storefront: a shop on a live stream

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    New in Rutba Storefront: show screens put a shop's products on a live stream or a screen in the shop, one item at a time. At Larkspur Corner Store each published group in Product Groups has a Show link and a Social link, and the group's page lists both addresses on the shop's own domain. In site settings a Show Screens card picks what appears beside each item. Out of the box that is a QR code and a short order code; a WhatsApp order, a link to type, the offer's end date and the group's name stay off until the shop turns them on. On the show the right and left arrows step through the items, so a presenter's clicker or a streaming tool can drive it, and up and down step through an item's pictures. A switch can also be turned on for one window from the show's address: here it adds a WhatsApp QR that opens a chat with the order already typed. A viewer who cannot scan types the code, 2V, into the shop's search and gets the apples at the price on the show. Larkspur Corner Store is an invented corner shop in Leeds, made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/commerce/storefront
