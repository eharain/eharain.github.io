    Rutba Employee Self-Service: the basics

    Published 4 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Employee Self-Service is each person's own corner of Rutba People: their leave, their payslips and their attendance, without asking the office. A short tour of the general manager's own view at Wrenmoor Garden Centre, an invented garden centre in north London: the start page with his team and his own record, his leave balance and requests, his payslips and one opened line by line, the clock for staff who work at a computer, his goal, his place in the organisation, and how a grievance goes straight to HR. Nothing is saved in the film. Wrenmoor Garden Centre is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/people/ess
