    Rutba Workforce: the basics

    Published 4 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba Workforce is where managers run the working week: the rota, the hours people work, their timesheets and their absence. A short tour with Wrenmoor Garden Centre, an invented garden centre in north London: the start page, this week's published rota and the rule that refuses a shift on a day of leave, four timesheets waiting for approval, attendance worked out from the clock punches, the November absence calendar, and the exceptions a supervisor answers before payroll runs. Wrenmoor Garden Centre is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/people/workforce
