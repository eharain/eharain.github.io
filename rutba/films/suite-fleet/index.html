    Rutba Fleet: the vans, the drivers, and who may take what

    Published 30 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Kilnside Builders runs four building crews out of a yard in Bristol, with seven vehicles from small vans to a crane lorry in Rutba Fleet. The start page shows which vehicles are legal to go out, and the dates that are due or already past. The tipper's insurance lapsed three days ago, so it cannot go out. Every driver's licence is on record, and when a driver with a car licence is asked to take the 7.5 tonne lorry, Fleet refuses the check-out and says why. A crew lead with the right licence hands back a van and takes the lorry instead. The film ends on the year's defects and the fuel. Kilnside Builders is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/fleet
