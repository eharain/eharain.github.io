    Rutba Point of Sale: an exchange balance paid back in cash

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    New in Rutba Point of Sale: when returned goods are worth more than the new sale, the till asks what happens to the balance. At Larkspur Corner Store a customer brings back a £3.75 sandwich and takes a £2.60 salad pot instead. Wale Dubois scans her receipt, ticks the sandwich, and at checkout the till shows a £1.15 exchange balance with two choices: pay back in cash or no cash back. Nothing is picked for him. He pays it back in cash, the paid sale lists the cash paid back, and his register records it as a refund, so the cash expected in the drawer falls from £91.09 to £89.94. Before this change the balance was shown as unused credit and cash handed back left no record. Larkspur Corner Store is an invented corner shop in Leeds, made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/commerce/pos
