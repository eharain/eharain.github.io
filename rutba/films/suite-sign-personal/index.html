    Rutba Sign for a person: understand the paper, then draft it for the place it is for

    Published 30 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    The public Rutba Sign site is written for the person holding the paper. Its guides explain which documents can be signed electronically and which belong on paper, and what makes a signature hold up if anyone challenges it. The drafting wizard starts from the place a document is for and offers only the documents written for that place, so a deed under English law is flagged as paper only before a word is typed. A private car sale is started on the first screen, and nothing typed there leaves the browser. Rutba Sign is in early access. https://sign.rutba.io
