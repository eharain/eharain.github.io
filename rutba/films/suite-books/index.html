    Rutba Books: the money side of a trading business

    Published 30 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Northbank Trading supplies cafes, delis and grocers, and keeps its books in Rutba Books. A customer pays an invoice, the payment is recorded against it, and the money owed to the business goes down on the start page. Then the supplier bills and the payments that settle them, the monthly running costs, the bank account and the cash flow for the weeks ahead. The film ends on the income statement for the year so far. Northbank Trading is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/books
