    Rutba Payroll: the basics

    Published 4 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba Payroll pays a business's staff, on a salary or a day rate, and posts every run to the accounts. A short tour with Wrenmoor Garden Centre, an invented garden centre in north London: the start page with October's run and eight months of pay costs, the list of monthly runs and how a run goes from a dry run to paid, October's payslips, each person's pay profile, the deduction rules, and adjustments such as bonuses and loans. The deduction rates on this sample are examples, not real UK tax rates. Nothing is saved or paid in the film. Wrenmoor Garden Centre is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/people/payroll
