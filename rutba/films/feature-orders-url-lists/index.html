    Rutba Order Management: share a list as you filtered it

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    New in the Rutba apps: a list keeps its filters and its page number in the address. At Saffron Lane Pizza and Curry, Fatima Cole has the 40 cancelled orders open in Order Management. When she moves to page two the address ends in page=2, a reload keeps her there, and the browser's Back button takes her to page one. Before this change a reload went back to page one. In Cash drops she picks Verified and every payment method, and both choices go into the address, so she can paste it into a message to the accounts team. A colleague who opens it, signed in with access to Orders, sees the same list with the same filters. The lists in the other Rutba apps work the same way, Stock, Books, HR and Help Desk among them. Nothing is saved or changed in the film. Saffron Lane Pizza and Curry is an invented takeaway in south London, made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/orders/orders
