    Rutba Admin: run your organisation's own console

    Published 31 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Rutba Admin is the console an organisation uses to run its own Rutba instance. In this film Northbank Trading's administrator gives a new starter the Warehouse job title: the console shows exactly which app roles it would add and take away before anything changes, and the new starter can open the warehouse apps a moment later. Then a walk through the rest of the console: which apps the organisation holds and has switched on, its domains and email sender, its phone numbers, its own payment accounts, its webhooks, and how the instance is recovered if nobody can administer it. Northbank Trading is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io
