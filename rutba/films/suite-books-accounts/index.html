    Rutba Accounts: the basics

    Published 4 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba Accounts is the accountant's side of the books Rutba Books keeps: the chart of accounts, the journal, the general ledger and the reports, with every invoice, receipt, bill and payment already posted as an entry. A short tour with Northbank Trading, an invented wholesaler in London: the start page, the chart of accounts and its balances, the journal with one customer payment opened up to its debit and credit, a month of the bank account in the general ledger, this year's income statement, the balance sheet, the VAT rates and the financial year, and what closing last year would post. Nothing is saved or closed in the film. Northbank Trading is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/books/accounts
