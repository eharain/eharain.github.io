    Rutba People: your team, paid and looked after

    Published 30 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Wrenmoor Garden Centre, with its plant nursery and cafe, runs its staff in Rutba People. A leave request is approved in HR, a tablet is paired as the staff clock, and a supervisor clocks in with her PIN. Her attendance shows in HR straight away. Then the year's pay runs, the payslips staff see for themselves, a Christmas vacancy on its hiring board, and the review cycle. Wrenmoor Garden Centre is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/people
