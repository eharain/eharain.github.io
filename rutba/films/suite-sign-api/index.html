    Rutba Sign API: send an agreement from your own system, then prove it

    Published 31 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Rutba Sign has an API for developers: everything the app does when sending, under the business's own key. In this film Kilnside Builders' quoting system sends a quote to sign with one call, the customer signs in person on the builder's tablet, and the system reads back the status, the evidence ledger and the sealed evidence pack. The pack's manifest hashes to the record's digest, and the signed copy checks out on the public verify page. No key can sign: signing stays each party's own act. Kilnside Builders is an invented business made for this film; every call ran for real against a local copy. https://sign.rutba.io/developers
