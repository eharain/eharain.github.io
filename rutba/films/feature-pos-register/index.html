    Rutba Point of Sale: reading and closing a register

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    New in Rutba Point of Sale: a cash register now reads like a statement. At Larkspur Corner Store, the owner, Imogen Okafor, opens Wale's register on Till 1 after a day of cash, card and mobile wallet sales. One tile per payment method sits across the top. The drawer card works out the cash that should be there, cash back included. Below it the ledger lists every event with what the drawer held after it. She closes the register from the list, leaves fifty pounds as the next day's float and draws the rest out to the safe. Closing is for a manager or the owner; till staff cannot close a register. Larkspur Corner Store is an invented corner shop in Leeds, made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/commerce/pos
