    Rutba Warehouse Control: the basics

    Published 2 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Warehouse Control is the warehouse half of Rutba Inventory: where the stock sits, the paperwork that moves it, counts, losses and reordering. A short tour with Northbank Trading, an invented wholesaler in London with one warehouse: the start page, the warehouse and its 191 storage locations, stock by location, four recorded losses, a reorder suggestion and the stock valuation. Transfers, cycle counts, batches and expiry have screens of their own and are empty for this business. Northbank Trading is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/inventory/control
