    Rutba Quality: the basics

    Published 9 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba Quality keeps every inspection a workshop makes and every fault found. A short tour with Granite and Grain, an invented furniture workshop in Leeds: first-pass yield over thirty days, the verdicts and where failures happen, the list of inspections filtered to rework, and a non-conformance from being raised to being closed. Inspection plans, holds, corrective actions and supplier ratings are named and not shown, because this sample has not started using them. Granite and Grain is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/manufacturing/quality
