    Rutba Stock: the basics

    Published 2 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba Stock keeps a business's product list, the stock it holds, and the purchases that bring more in. A short tour with Northbank Trading, an invented wholesaler in London: the start page and its charts, the product list, one product's record and its 70 stock items, the purchase orders and one received order, and the suppliers. Every unit is tracked on its own, from the order that brought it in. Northbank Trading is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/inventory/stock
