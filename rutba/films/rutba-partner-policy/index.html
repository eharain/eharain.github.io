    The Rutba partner policy, in plain terms

    Published 27 Oct 2026 · Partners

    The Rutba partner policy, section by section, from the page Tech Style published on 27 October 2026. One policy covers partners.rutba.io and tech-style.co.

    It covers who can partner, the four tracks, and how a partner's band is set and reviewed every six months. Targets are agreed in writing before your first introduction. Exclusivity is for a fixed term against a guaranteed minimum, and individuals are never exclusive. It also explains renewals and account care, deal registration, and when fees are earned and paid.

    There is nothing to pay to join and nothing to stock. Anything committed up front is optional and only buys a higher band. Training and certification are free. The rates themselves are confidential and are sent on request once approved.

    Read the policy: https://www.tech-style.co/partners/policy Apply: https://partners.rutba.io
