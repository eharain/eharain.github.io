    Rutba CRM: the basics

    Published 2 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba CRM keeps a business's customers and leads in one place, with every call, email and meeting on the record. A short tour of the basics with Northbank Trading, an invented wholesaler in London that supplies dry goods to cafes, delis, hotels and caterers: the start page and its charts, the leads board, one lead and its timeline, follow-ups, contacts, the activity log, and a customer's page with their Helpdesk tickets and orders.
