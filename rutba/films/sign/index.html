    Rutba Sign: the signature is the easy part, the evidence is the product

    Published 19 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Anyone can put a picture of a signature on a page. What settles a dispute later is the record behind it.

    Rutba Sign puts every step of an eSignature on a sealed, hash-chained record, timestamped by a public authority and countersigned by Rutba. The people you send to need no account and are never charged. Anyone can verify a completed record for free, without an account, even if the sender stops paying.

    Signing and verifying are always free, and you pay only to send. It is in EarlyAccess now.
