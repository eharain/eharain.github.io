    Rutba HR: the basics

    Published 4 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba HR keeps the staff records for a business: who works there, who they report to, their attendance and their time off. A short tour with Wrenmoor Garden Centre, an invented garden centre in north London with a plant nursery and a cafe: the start page, the staff list, the org chart and what a reporting line lets a manager approve, one person's employment history, today's attendance from the clock punches, the leave requests waiting for a decision and the approved ones, and People Analytics with attrition, time to fill, absence and why people left. Nothing is saved in the film. Wrenmoor Garden Centre is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/people/hr
