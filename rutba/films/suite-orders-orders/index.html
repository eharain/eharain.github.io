    Rutba Order Management: the basics

    Published 3 Nov 2026 · How-to guides

    Rutba Order Management follows web orders from the moment they are placed to the customer's door. A short tour with Saffron Lane Pizza and Curry, an invented takeaway in south London: the day's figures and charts, the order board and its stages, the order list, a delivered order and one paid in cash at the door, the calls to confirm, the cash waiting to be checked, and the riders, zones and delivery prices. Nothing is saved or moved in the film. Saffron Lane Pizza and Curry is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/orders/orders
