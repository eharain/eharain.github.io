    Rutba Facilities: the workshop, its things and the people who fix them

    Published 30 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Granite and Grain makes furniture in a leased unit in Leeds, and keeps track of its machines, tools, keys and van in Rutba Assets. Each asset has an owner, a place, a history and the warranty or insurance that covers it, and the big machines are linked to their records in the books. Rutba Facilities holds the lease, the rooms, the contractors and their insurance. When the showroom lights fail, the job is raised as an order, and an electrician whose insurance has lapsed is turned away before the work is booked. Granite and Grain is an invented business made for this film. https://rutba.io/suites/facilities
