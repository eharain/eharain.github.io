    How to use the free JSON tool: minify, compress, round trip, validate

    Published 19 Oct 2026 · How-to guides

    A one-minute recording of the free JSON tool, used for real.

    Paste your own JSON or open a file. Compress writes each key name once and turns records into columns and rows: on the 60-record sample it came out 69% smaller than minified. Decompress gives back exactly what went in, and validation names the line and column where a broken document fails.

    It all runs in your browser, and nothing you paste is uploaded.
