    Paritas: become a development partner, and your migration comes first

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Partners

    Paritas is an Oracle-compatible database built on PostgreSQL, with the tools to move your applications onto it. We are inviting organisations to become our development partners. On 10 November 2026, Paritas gave Oracle's answer to 690 of the 829 cases we run on Oracle Database 21c. The rest is the work ahead, and we would rather do it on real applications. A development partner tells us what its estate runs on, and runs pgrecon on it. pgrecon is available now: a read-only script your DBA runs, which reports each finding by object and line. Then we test together, with your own tests run on Oracle and on Paritas side by side. In return, we focus on our partners' migrations first. We tell you what we can measure for your code, and we are happy to show you the test harness running live. Where your code finds a gap, we write it down, with a way round where there is one. Paritas is not for sale yet, and we are aiming to release it next quarter. To become a development partner, write to hello@paritas.tech and tell us what your estate runs on. https://paritas.tech/ Oracle DatabaseMigration PLSQL Database
