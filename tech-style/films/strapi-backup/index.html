    Strapi Remote Backup Pro: back up Strapi without installing a plugin, in development

    Published 19 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Backing up a Strapi site usually means installing a plugin and redeploying. Strapi Remote Backup Pro runs outside the CMS instead: point it at a URL and sign in as an admin.

    Back up everything, or pick content types and records, and it follows their relations so nothing is left dangling. It has been proved on Strapi v5, with a backup restored into another live site and checked record by record.

    The engine and CLI are on npm now as 0.1.0, MIT licensed. The desktop app is not built yet.
