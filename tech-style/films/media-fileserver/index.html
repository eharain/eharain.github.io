    Rutba Media FileServer: store masters only, resize on request

    Published 19 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Rendering every image size at upload turned 5,000 originals into 30,000 files, most of them never requested.

    Rutba Media FileServer stores the masters only. Variants are made when a browser asks for them, cached, and aged out when the design changes. Add a database and it becomes a full asset manager, or leave it out and the origin behaves as before. It runs in production, and from a cold start it answers over 150,000 cached files in under a second. There is a drop-in Strapi upload provider.

    It is OpenSource under the AGPL.
