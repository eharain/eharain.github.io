    Tech Style: who can be a referral partner

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Partners

    Both individuals and organisations can be referral partners with Tech Style. An individual is a person selling on their own account, such as an agent, consultant, journalist or freelancer; an organisation is a registered business, with someone who can sign for it. Individuals work on the referral track only. No history with us is needed: the Registered band is for new individuals, and new organisations on the referral track, and there is nothing to pay to join and nothing to stock. What the track asks for is a real introduction, to a named person expecting our call, and any target is agreed in writing before your first introduction. A referral fee is paid on net revenue, earned when the customer pays and worked out once the refund period has passed. The rates are confidential; your own rate is the one in your written agreement. The whole policy: https://tech-style.co/partners/policy
