    pgrecon: free, open-source Oracle to PostgreSQL migration reconnaissance

    Published 22 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Before an Oracle to PostgreSQL migration, one question decides the budget: what is actually in your Oracle code?

    pgrecon answers it without connecting to your database. Your own DBA runs a read-only extraction script, and the analysis happens offline. 80 rules across nine areas report each finding with its severity and the object it was found in. The effort estimate comes in person-days with the arithmetic shown, so your team can argue with any line of it.

    It converts the schema it can prove and names everything it will not convert. It is free under Apache 2.0, still alpha, and on PyPI.
