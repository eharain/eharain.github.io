    Paritas: where Oracle compatibility ends, as we measured it

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    Paritas is an Oracle-compatible database built on PostgreSQL. This film shows where its compatibility ends. On 10 November 2026 we ran the 829 cases that Oracle Database 21c answers: Paritas gave Oracle's answer to 690 of them (83%), and the unmodified open-source base it is built from gave 520. Some differences are still open, and they give no error. Ten divided by four is 2 on Paritas today, where Oracle gives 2.5; write one side as a decimal and both give 2.5. A quotient keeps 17 to 20 significant digits, where Oracle keeps 38 to 40; round to the digits that matter and the answer is the same. GREATEST of 5 and NULL gives 5 on Paritas and NULL on Oracle; test for the NULL first with a CASE. Our list of limitations runs to more than 900 entries, and most say what to do instead. Paritas is not for sale yet, and we are aiming to release it next quarter.
