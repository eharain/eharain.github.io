    Tech Style partners: appointed where they are based

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Partners

    We appoint a partner for the territory where it is based and works locally, not across territories. A firm in India that wants to sell in the Middle East is offered India. This film shows where the policy says so, the "Territory where you are based" field on the form, and the 30-minute call that follows an application, where we talk about your market, the lines you want, what we have already sold there and which accounts are excluded. Being appointed does not make a territory exclusive: exclusivity is open only on the Invested band, by country and by line, and individuals are never exclusive. The regions we are appointing in now are in the partner programme film: https://youtu.be/zdtMRAXUd2E. The whole policy: https://tech-style.co/partners/policy
