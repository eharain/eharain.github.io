    Rutba MTA: open-source email relay middleware

    Published 22 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    Every application that sends email ends up rebuilding the same deliverability work. Rutba MTA does it once, between your applications and your own SMTP servers.

    Final delivery always goes through your own server, so the sending reputation stays yours. Each receiving domain gets a live reputation score, and marketing mail slows down when a domain starts to push back. Suppressed addresses never reach the queue, and a password reset always overtakes a newsletter. Applications get a small API with signed webhooks.

    It is OpenSource under the AGPL, with a commercial licence available.
