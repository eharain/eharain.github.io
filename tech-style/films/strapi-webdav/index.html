    A WebDAV upload provider for Strapi v5

    Published 19 Oct 2026 · Introductions

    The Strapi media library does not have to live in a cloud bucket.

    This upload provider points it at WebDAV storage you already have, such as a NAS or your own server. Install it from npm, set it as the upload provider and give it your WebDAV address, and the media library stores its files there.

    For Strapi v5, version 1.0.2, free and MIT licensed.
