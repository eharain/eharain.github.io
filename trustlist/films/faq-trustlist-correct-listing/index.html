    Is your TrustList listing wrong? Claim it, or write to us

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    If your company's listing on TrustList is wrong, you can claim it and correct it yourself, or write to us. Both are free. Every listing page has a link, Own this listing? Claim it, which opens the claim form in the Growth Hub with your listing already chosen. You need a free account, with no card. If your email is on your company's own website domain, one click in our email makes you an owner straight away. If not, a person checks the claim, usually in 1 to 2 business days. As an owner you correct the listing in the editor, from the name and description to the categories, contacts and addresses, and you publish the changes yourself after the same checks as a new listing. If the person who set it up has left, anyone else at the company can claim it too. That adds them as an owner and removes nobody. You can also write to us from the contact page and choose A listing (adding, updating or correcting). We would rather correct a listing than retire it. The company in this film, Fernhollow Data Studio, is made up for our demonstration films. Contact page: https://trustlist.uk/contact-us
