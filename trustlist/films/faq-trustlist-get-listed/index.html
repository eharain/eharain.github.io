    Get your company listed on TrustList

    Published 20 Nov 2026 · Introductions

    Adding your company to TrustList is free. Create a free account, open the Growth Hub, choose Add or claim, then Register a company. If your company is already listed, claim it instead. You confirm the listing guidelines first: TrustList lists business software and the technology services bought alongside it, and does not list shops, gambling or consumer finance. A new listing starts as a draft only you can see. You publish it yourself once it has a description of at least 120 characters, 1 to 20 categories and a working website, and the business is in scope. Rankings are ordered by the TrustList Score, out of 100, from facts we can check. Paying never changes rank: sponsored cards are labelled and sit outside the ranked list, and no listing is first on more than five rankings. Rankings come in quarterly editions; Q4 2026 is live and the next is due in January 2027. The company in the film is a draft made for it and never published. Listing guidelines: https://trustlist.uk/listing-guidelines How rankings work: https://trustlist.uk/best/methodology
